# Example — Role comparison

This example is fictional.

## Profile evidence

- Senior Product Designer for 5 years.
- Owns end-to-end flows.
- Works with Product and Engineering.
- Maintains a design system.
- Mentors one junior designer.

## Target role

Staff Product Designer role asking for:

- influence across multiple product teams;
- facilitation of ambiguous product decisions;
- design-system contribution;
- mentoring;
- executive communication;
- measurable product outcomes.

## Looking Glass comparison

### Strong alignment

**Direct Evidence**
- End-to-end product design.
- Cross-functional collaboration.
- Design-system ownership.
- Mentoring.

### Partial alignment

**Inference**
- The current evidence may support leadership potential, but cross-team influence is not yet visible.
- Ambiguous problem framing may exist in practice, but it is not demonstrated in the supplied profile.

### Missing or weakly visible evidence

The supplied material does not currently show:

- influence across multiple teams;
- executive-level communication;
- measurable outcomes;
- responsibility for product direction beyond one team.

### Important distinction

These are **narrative evidence gaps**, not proof that the person lacks the experience.

### Direction

Before changing the headline to emphasize Staff-level scope, gather and surface concrete examples of:

- decisions that affected more than one team;
- product ambiguity the person helped resolve;
- system-level contributions;
- measurable or observable impact.
