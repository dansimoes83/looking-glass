# Using Looking Glass in any AI conversation

You do not need to create an assistant, GPT, Gem, or project.

## Minimal setup

Provide:

1. `instructions/looking-glass-instructions.md`
2. the professional material
3. the goal

Then ask for a diagnosis.

Example:

> Use Looking Glass as the framework for this conversation. Review the attached résumé and portfolio introduction. I want to understand what professional identity they currently communicate. Do not rewrite anything yet.

## For a role comparison

Add the role description:

> Compare the supplied material against this role. Separate direct evidence from inference, and distinguish narrative gaps from experience gaps.

## For a later rewrite

After reviewing the diagnosis:

> Move to rewrite mode. Preserve my voice and only change the parts directly supported by the diagnosis.

This keeps diagnosis and intervention separate.
