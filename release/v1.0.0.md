# Looking Glass v1.0.0

**Diagnose before writing.**

This is the first public, model-agnostic release of Looking Glass.

Looking Glass helps people understand how their professional experience is currently being perceived before rewriting a LinkedIn profile, résumé, portfolio, bio, or related career narrative.

## What is included

- canonical framework;
- canonical AI instructions;
- evidence model;
- adaptive diagnostic flow;
- rewrite checkpoint;
- ChatGPT guide;
- Gemini guide;
- generic conversation guide;
- Cursor guide;
- Codex guide;
- fictional examples for LinkedIn, portfolio, and role comparison.

## What changed from the earlier agent concept

The earlier Looking Glass agent was centered on LinkedIn strategy and career transition.

This release separates the durable method from any specific platform.

Looking Glass is now defined as a **professional narrative diagnostic framework**.

LinkedIn is one surface, not the product.

AI is one execution environment, not the method.

## Core principles

- Diagnose before writing.
- Preserve identity over optimization.
- Never invent evidence.
- Never exaggerate seniority.
- Declare inference.
- Do not pretend to see what cannot be observed.
