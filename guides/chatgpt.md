# Using Looking Glass with ChatGPT

Looking Glass is model-agnostic. ChatGPT is one possible environment.

## Persistent use

Use `instructions/looking-glass-instructions.md` as persistent project or assistant instructions when the environment supports them.

Then add the professional material you want Looking Glass to review.

Suggested first request:

> Use Looking Glass to diagnose how these materials are currently positioning me. Do not rewrite anything yet.

## Conversation use

If you do not want a persistent setup:

1. Attach or paste `looking-glass-instructions.md`.
2. Attach or paste the professional material.
3. State the goal.

Example:

> Use the attached Looking Glass instructions. Review my LinkedIn About and recent experience. I want to understand whether I currently read more as a senior executor or as a staff-level product designer. Diagnosis only.

## Good inputs

- LinkedIn text export or pasted sections;
- résumé or CV;
- portfolio copy;
- job description;
- writing samples, when voice matters.

## Important

The specific UI for projects, assistants, or persistent instructions may change over time.

The durable rule is simple:

> Give the model the canonical Looking Glass instruction file as persistent instructions or conversation context.
